<section class="hero">
  <div><p class="eyebrow">City–AI Coevolution</p><h1>Understanding cities shaped by AI.</h1>
  <p class="lead">CAIO is is a five-year research project (2026–2031) funded with €1.4 million by the <i>Italian Ministry of Research (MUR)</i> through a <a href="https://fis-submission.mur.gov.it/">Fondo Italiano per la Scienza (FIS)</a> <strong>Consolidator Grant</strong> programme.</p> 
    <p>It studies how algorithmic recommendations and urban life co-evolve, and designs AI systems that work for people, cities, and the public good.</p>
  </div>
</section>
<section class="section-band">
  <div class="section-intro"><h2>Three research pillars</h2><p>From measuring emerging urban dynamics to designing responsible, complexity-aware systems.</p></div>
  <div class="grid">
    <article class="card"><span class="card-number">01</span><h3>Understand</h3><p>Measure how AI-driven recommenders affect mobility, sustainability and inequality.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><span class="card-number">02</span><h3>Model</h3><p>Build mechanistic models that reveal the dynamics of city–AI feedback loops.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><span class="card-number">03</span><h3>Govern</h3><p>Design complexity-informed recommenders aligned with collective urban goals.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
<section class="statement"><p>Tomorrow’s cities will not be shaped by people or algorithms alone, but by the feedback loops between them.</p></section>
