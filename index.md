  <div><p class="eyebrow">City–AI Coevolution</p><h1>Understanding cities shaped by AI.</h1>
  <p class="lead">CAIO is a five-year research project (2026–2031) funded with €1.4 million by the <i>Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR)</i> through a <a href="https://fis-submission.mur.gov.it/">Fondo Italiano per la Scienza (FIS)</a> <strong>Consolidator Grant</strong>.</p>
    <p>It studies how algorithmic recommendations and urban life co-evolve, and designs AI systems that work for people, cities, and the public good.</p>
